# P4.1.1 — Full-bleed homepage hero

This pass keeps the P4.1 homepage structure but changes the first impression from a split text/card hero to a cinematic full-width hero.

## Why

The P4.1 homepage had a stronger campaign structure, but the hero still felt like a premium template because the product image lived inside a rounded card. Neuvago needs a more ownable opening moment: warm, cinematic, full-width, and closer to a brand campaign.

## What changed

- The homepage hero image is now full-bleed and spans the full viewport width.
- The copy sits over the image on a warm cream gradient veil.
- The product/app scene remains on the right side of the composition.
- The hero remains video-ready: the media layer can later be replaced with an ambient loop and poster fallback.
- CTAs and the calm proof chips remain visible without turning the hero into a hard sales section.

## What did not change

- No routes changed.
- No SEO architecture changed.
- No content cluster changes were made.
- No video has been added yet.

## QA focus

After deploy, check:

1. Desktop homepage topfold: image should feel cinematic and full-width, not like a card.
2. Mobile homepage topfold: text should remain readable and the image crop should not feel cramped.
3. LCP/performance: still image should remain acceptable before any future video work.
4. Visual transition into the proof strip and system reveal.

## Follow-up polish

P4.1.2 refined the mobile headline, strengthened the warm text veil, simplified the hero glass panel, and aligned the header CTA with the “system” language.
