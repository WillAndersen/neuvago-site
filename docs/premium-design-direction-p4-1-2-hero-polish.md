# P4.1.2 — Homepage hero polish

This pass keeps the full-bleed P4.1.1 homepage hero, but refines the details that showed up in screenshot QA.

## Why

The full-bleed hero direction is correct. It makes the homepage feel more like a premium brand campaign and less like a split-layout template. The screenshot review still showed a few polish issues:

- The mobile headline felt slightly tight.
- The mobile image needed a stronger warm veil behind the headline for readability.
- The hero glass panel repeated the proof-strip messaging too much.
- The header CTA could align better with the new “system” language.

## What changed

- Mobile headline sizing, tracking, and line-height were refined.
- The mobile gradient veil is stronger and warmer, while staying non-clinical and light.
- The hero proof panel is simplified and shown only from tablet/desktop upward.
- The hero panel now says “Device + app + routine” instead of repeating all proof chips.
- The desktop header CTA now says “Explore system” instead of “Explore product”.

## What did not change

- No routes changed.
- No SEO architecture changed.
- No image assets changed.
- No video has been added yet.
- The full-bleed hero composition remains the homepage direction.

## QA focus

After deploy, check:

1. Mobile topfold: headline should feel intentional, not cramped.
2. Mobile topfold: headline should remain readable over the image.
3. Desktop topfold: the hero should still feel cinematic and full-width.
4. Proof strip: should not feel duplicated by the hero panel.
5. Header CTA: should feel aligned with the product-system positioning.
