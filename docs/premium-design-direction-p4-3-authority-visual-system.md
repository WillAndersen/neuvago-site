# P4.3 Authority visual system

This pass lifts Learn, Research and Conditions from a template-like SEO library into a more premium editorial authority system.

## Goals

- Make Learn feel like a calm education library.
- Make Research feel like a restrained evidence layer.
- Make Conditions feel like practical everyday pathways.
- Add plain-English summaries to the key research and VNS pages.
- Add a research ledger pattern that separates evidence, limitations and Neuvago boundaries.
- Add routine pathway sections for stress and sleep.

## Patterns added

- `AuthorityEditorialHero`
- `PlainEnglishSummary`
- `ResearchLedger`
- `AuthorityPathways`
- `ConditionRoutinePathway`

## Pages touched

- `/learn`
- `/research`
- `/conditions`
- VNS Learn pages
- VNS/tVNS/safety/autonomic/HRV research topic pages
- `/conditions/stress`
- `/conditions/sleep`

## What this does not do

- It does not add new routes.
- It does not change domain/canonical strategy.
- It does not add Product schema with offers/reviews/ratings.
- It does not rewrite legal pages or study pages.
