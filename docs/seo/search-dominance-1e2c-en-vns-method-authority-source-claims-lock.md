## Locked claim boundaries
- `VNS` is not one protocol or one device category.
- Implanted VNS evidence and labeling do not transfer automatically to non-invasive methods.
- `tVNS` is a method family; auricular and cervical approaches are not interchangeable.
- `taVNS` is ear-based tVNS, but the outer ear is not uniformly vagally innervated.
- Anatomical plausibility does not by itself prove target engagement.
- Sensation does not prove vagal engagement or clinical efficacy.
- Frequency, pulse width, waveform, intensity, duty cycle, session length and total exposure matter.
- Sham/control site and blinding matter.
- Healthy-volunteer, clinical-population and wellness-user evidence are not interchangeable.
- HRV, imaging, pupil, symptom and clinical outcomes are not interchangeable measures of target engagement.
- Safety/tolerability findings stay tied to the actual protocol and population.
- General VNS/tVNS/taVNS evidence is METHOD EVIDENCE or GENERAL PHYSIOLOGY unless it is specific to Neuvago.
- No external device or protocol result may be presented as Neuvago product efficacy.
- Neuvago remains within the controlled intended-use and regulatory fact gates.
