## RESET-VID-01 — “Can you reset the vagus nerve?”

**Owner:** `/learn/vagus-nerve-reset`

**Primary format:** 60–90 second 16:9 explainer with 9:16 cutdown.

**Narrative**
1. Open with the exact question: “Can you reset the vagus nerve?”
2. Answer immediately: not literally.
3. Show the difference between a **felt shift**, a **measured change**, and an assumed **mechanism**.
4. Explain that seven minutes is a memorable routine length, not proof of a universal biological reset.
5. End with the practical frame: choose a safe regulation practice and judge the outcome actually experienced.

**Allowed visual language**
- calm state-transition / pacing graphics,
- breathing / rest / reduced-input metaphors,
- generic HR/HRV measurement context,
- “reset ≠ literal switch” conceptual typography.

**Do not show**
- a literal anatomical reset button,
- a countdown that implies guaranteed effect,
- “before/after vagus score”,
- disease-treatment promises,
- Neuvago as the proof behind generic reset claims.

## MASSAGE-VID-01 — “Can you massage the vagus nerve?”

**Owner:** `/learn/vagus-nerve-massage`

**Primary format:** 75–105 second 16:9 explainer with 9:16 cutdown.

**Narrative**
1. Answer immediately: you can massage skin/muscle, not literally reach and massage the cervical vagus as a superficial cord.
2. Separate gentle touch, muscle massage, ear touch/acupressure, clinician-only carotid sinus massage, and electrical VNS.
3. State that ear touch is not electrical taVNS.
4. State the key safety boundary: no strong/deep pressure, percussion or device-assisted work over the front/side of the neck; carotid sinus massage is not a home technique.
5. End with evidence interpretation: outcome ≠ proof of selective vagus engagement.

**Do not show**
- finger-placement coordinates over the carotid sinus,
- a “vagus pressure point” target,
- deep-neck self-massage instruction,
- a massage gun on the front/side neck,
- manual touch represented as electrical taVNS.
