## Reset claim boundaries

Allowed:

- “Reset” is popular shorthand / metaphor rather than a literal anatomical reset.
- A short routine may help a person feel calmer without proving a vagus-specific mechanism.
- Slow breathing can affect heart rate, HRV-related measures and experience under studied conditions.
- One HR/HRV value does not diagnose vagus function or prove a reset.
- Electrical VNS is a distinct device/protocol category.

Not allowed:

- The vagus nerve can literally be reset on command.
- One universal seven-minute routine is validated or guaranteed.
- A wearable value proves a weak, damaged, inflamed or “unbalanced” vagus nerve.
- General breathing, grounding, movement or relaxation is automatically electrical VNS.
- Category-level VNS evidence becomes Neuvago product evidence.

## Massage claim and safety boundaries

Allowed:

- People can massage skin and muscle, but that is not the same as directly massaging the cervical vagus nerve.
- The outer ear has mixed innervation; manual ear touch is not equivalent to electrical taVNS.
- Massage studies can report subjective and cardiac/autonomic measures without proving selective vagus engagement.
- Pressure-point traditions and acupressure outcomes do not anatomically validate one universal “vagus point.”
- Strong/deep front- or side-neck pressure, carotid-sinus self-massage and device-assisted percussion/vibration there are outside this general-wellness guide.

Not allowed:

- Give carotid-sinus self-massage instructions, coordinates, force, duration or side choice.
- Give a consumer palpation target for the cervical vagus.
- Present dizziness, pulse change, pain or neurological symptoms as desirable evidence of “vagus activation.”
- Treat manual massage or acupressure evidence as Neuvago product evidence.
- Transfer electrical taVNS findings directly to manual ear massage.
