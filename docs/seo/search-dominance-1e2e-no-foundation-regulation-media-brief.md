## VN-001 — `/no/kunnskap/vagusnerven`

### VN-NO-VID-01 — «Vagusnerven på 90 sekunder»
Explain cranial nerve X, left/right vagus nerves, the broad brainstem-to-neck-to-thoracoabdominal course, mixed fibre types and the distinction between anatomy, HRV, behavioural regulation and electrical VNS.

### VN-NO-IMG-01 — «Hvor går vagusnerven?»
Editorial anatomy diagram showing brainstem, paired cervical course, thorax and upper abdominal branching at a high level.

Do not show a single surface pressure point, a literal calm switch, a universal organ-control cable or a DIY stimulation target.

## ANS-001 — `/no/kunnskap/det-autonome-nervesystemet`

### ANS-NO-VID-01 — «Det autonome nervesystemet: mer enn stress og ro»
Explain sympathetic, parasympathetic and enteric regulation as interacting systems with organ-specific effects.

### ANS-NO-IMG-01 — «Sympatisk, parasympatisk og enterisk regulering»
Conceptual diagram that makes coexistence and coordination visible without presenting a 50/50 balance meter.

Do not depict sympathetic activity as bad, parasympathetic activity as always good, or one wearable score as the state of the whole ANS.

## ANS-002 — `/no/kunnskap/det-parasympatiske-nervesystemet`

### PARA-NO-VID-01 — «Hva gjør det parasympatiske nervesystemet?»
Explain the craniosacral organisation, organ-specific actions, the vagus nerve's important but limited role, and why “rest and digest” is an introduction rather than a complete model.

### PARA-NO-IMG-01 — «Vagusnerven er ikke hele det parasympatiske systemet»
High-level anatomy/taxonomy visual distinguishing cranial vagal pathways from other cranial and sacral parasympathetic pathways.

Do not imply that all parasympathetic activity is vagal or that the system can be switched on globally.

## ANS-008 — `/no/kunnskap/hvordan-roe-ned-nervesystemet`

### CALM-NO-VID-01 — «Fem trygge innganger til nedregulering»
A calm, practical sequence covering situation/needs check, reducing unnecessary demands, comfortable breathing, movement/muscle relaxation/orientation, and when professional help is more appropriate.

### CALM-NO-IMG-01 — «Hva trenger kroppen akkurat nå?»
Decision-flow visual separating practical down-regulation from sleep/food/hydration/pain needs, problem solving, safety, and medical assessment.

Do not promise a rapid nervous-system reset, diagnose an “overactive nervous system”, prescribe breath-hold dosing, or imply that one technique works for everyone.
