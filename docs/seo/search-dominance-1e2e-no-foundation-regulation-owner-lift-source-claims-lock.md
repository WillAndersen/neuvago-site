## Scientific / safety boundaries retained

- The vagus nerve is cranial nerve X and consists of left and right nerves with mixed sensory, motor and parasympathetic fibres; it is not one universal calm switch.
- The vagus nerve is not the whole parasympathetic nervous system and not the whole autonomic nervous system.
- Sympathetic and parasympathetic activity are not a simple good/bad or gas/brake binary.
- HRV and related cardiac measures can provide partial information under defined conditions; they do not measure or diagnose the full autonomic system or the whole vagus nerve.
- Practical calming/regulation guidance remains non-diagnostic. New, severe, persistent or medically concerning symptoms require appropriate assessment.
- Behavioural practices, touch, electrical VNS, product use and medical treatment remain separate intervention/evidence categories.
- General physiology or external VNS research is not product evidence for Neuvago.
- No new carotid-sinus, neck-pressure, electrical-stimulation, breathing-dose or other risky DIY protocol is introduced.
