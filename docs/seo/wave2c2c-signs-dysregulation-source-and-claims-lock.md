# Wave 2C.2C — source and claims lock

## Scope

This lock governs NO-016 at `/no/kunnskap/tegn-pa-dysregulert-nervesystem` and its reciprocal language discovery with `/learn/signs-of-a-dysregulated-nervous-system`.

## Recognition boundary

“Dysregulated nervous system” is used as ordinary-language recognition, not a diagnosis. Symptoms and lived experiences are non-specific. The page must not imply that one symptom, several checklist rows, HRV, pulse, a consumer wearable, or a subjective response proves autonomic dysfunction, vagal dysfunction, low vagal tone, disease, or a need for vagus nerve stimulation.

The accessible HTML checklist is a language and observation tool. It has no checklist result, score, severity band, threshold, diagnostic label, treatment recommendation, or minimum number of recognised signs.

## Alternative explanations

The article must keep multiple contributors open, including stress, sleep, pain, illness, medication, caffeine and other substances, mental-health conditions, neurodivergence, hormonal change, life events, sensory context, work organisation and combinations of these factors.

## Product boundary

Neuvago may appear only after non-device guidance, alternative explanations, assessment guidance and acute boundaries. A recognised pattern is not an indication for Neuvago. General VNS, taVNS, stress, sleep or autonomic-regulation research is not automatically Neuvago-specific evidence.

The page must not claim treatment, prevention, diagnosis, disease relief, guaranteed reset, cortisol normalisation, HRV normalisation, proof of target engagement, proof of vagal activation, or universal causation. It must state that stronger stimulation is not necessarily better.

## Assessment and acute boundary

New, persistent, increasing, function-limiting or worrying symptoms should be assessed by a qualified health professional. The Norwegian page must preserve the distinction between the GP, legevakt 116 117 and emergency help 113. Acute or potentially life-threatening symptoms must never be routed to product support or framed as a self-regulation problem.

## Editorial review

The article requires a named author, source reviewer, published date and modified date. Source review checks whether each factual statement is supported and whether the wording stays inside this claims lock. It does not replace individual clinical assessment.

## Language and internal-link ownership

NO-016 owns Norwegian recognition intent for `tegn på dysregulert nervesystem`. The regulation pillar owns the broader concept; the calming guide owns practical how-to intent; the stress and sleep pathways own broad assessment/context intent; the overstimulation page owns sensory and stimulus-load recognition.

The English and Norwegian pages may link reciprocally without being presented as word-for-word translations.

## Analytics and privacy

Only the existing How It Works and Product navigation events may fire from `signs-product-bridge`. They may contain only the established seven non-personal parameters. Checklist selections, symptoms, diagnostic labels, medical history, HRV values, stress scores, free text, email, account IDs, user IDs or health data must not be collected.

## Visual policy

No AI-generated ear anatomy, electrode-contact or instructional placement image may be used as a final asset. Any future Neuvago device depiction must preserve the compact palm-sized real-world scale.

## Repository boundary

The installation and audit package must not stage, commit, push or delete unrelated untracked work. It must preserve the existing analytics contract, package manifest, dependencies, robots rules and registry-generated sitemap architecture.
