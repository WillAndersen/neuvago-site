## Locked direct-answer boundary

The vagus nerve does not have a literal reset button that can be guaranteed in minutes. “Reset” is treated as popular or metaphorical language for an attempt to move from high activation toward a calmer or more regulated state. Breathing or other gentle practices may influence experience and physiological measures, but that is not the same as resetting a nerve or proving direct, selective vagal stimulation.

The dated targeted exact-phrase review did not identify an eligible clinical guideline, controlled trial, systematic review or registered protocol validating one universal seven-minute reset. This is a limited discovery result, not proof that no relevant study exists.
