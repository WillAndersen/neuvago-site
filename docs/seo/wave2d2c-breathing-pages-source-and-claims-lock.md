## Core answer boundary

> Calm, comfortable and controlled breathing can change respiratory rhythm and influence heart-rate variation and perceived activation. Research links slow-paced breathing with changes in cardiac HRV measures, but this does not prove that an exercise directly or selectively stimulates the vagus nerve. There is no single universally best breathing rhythm.

> Rolig, komfortabel og kontrollert pust kan endre pusterytmen og påvirke pulsvariasjon og opplevd aktivering. Forskning knytter langsom, styrt pust til endringer i kardiale HRV-mål, men dette beviser ikke at en øvelse direkte eller selektivt stimulerer vagusnerven. Det finnes heller ikke én universelt beste pusterytme.

The pages may explain slow/controlled breathing, longer-exhale claims, 4-7-8, HRV and RespHRV. They must not turn a change in breathing, subjective state or cardiac measurement into proof of direct selective vagus-nerve stimulation.

## Evidence conclusions

1. Slow-paced breathing is associated with changes in cardiac HRV-related measures across a large but heterogeneous evidence base.
2. Inhalation/exhalation-ratio findings are mixed. Some studies support longer exhalation, one tested equal breathing favourably, and other studies found no ratio effect. No universal best ratio is locked.
3. The targeted 4-7-8 review found a small and heterogeneous evidence base, not an absence of studies. It does not establish universal superiority, broad treatment efficacy or selective vagus engagement.
4. HRV/RSA/RespHRV require respiratory and measurement context. They are not a whole-body vagus score or consumer diagnosis.
5. Behavioural breathing and electrical VNS remain separate method categories. External evidence does not transfer automatically to Neuvago.

## Practical boundary

Optional examples may be included only as gentle, comfortable, non-forced practices. A reader can stop, shorten or return to normal breathing. Breath holds are optional and must not be framed as a challenge. No hyperventilation, maximal-depth or “push through” instruction is allowed.
