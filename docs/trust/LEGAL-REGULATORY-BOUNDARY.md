# Legal/regulatory boundary

This implementation creates editorial governance and removes obvious public internal-draft placeholders. It does not provide legal advice, determine medical-device classification, certify CE conformity, determine FDA status, replace market-specific regulatory/legal review, or invent reviewers/approvals.

Rows marked OWNER_CONFIRMATION_REQUIRED, DOC_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED or FACT_GATED stay open until a real designated owner verifies them against current controlled documentation.
