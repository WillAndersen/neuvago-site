# NEUVAGO SEARCH DOMINANCE 1D — Claim Governance v1

## Evidence layers
1. PRODUCT EVIDENCE — Neuvago-specific evidence or controlled Neuvago product documentation.
2. METHOD EVIDENCE — evidence for a defined intervention such as implanted VNS, cervical nVNS, tVNS or auricular taVNS.
3. GENERAL PHYSIOLOGY — anatomy, autonomic mechanisms, associations and hypotheses.

## Global modality lock
Implanted VNS, cervical nVNS, tVNS, auricular taVNS and Neuvago are not interchangeable. Evidence, indications, safety conclusions, regulatory status, protocol parameters and outcomes do not transfer automatically.

## Review decisions
APPROVE_AS_WRITTEN
APPROVE_WITH_MINOR_EDITS
REVISE_AND_RESUBMIT
DO_NOT_RELEASE

## Deterministic change request
Record route, section ID, exact current text, exact replacement/deletion, reason, supporting source or professional rationale and severity.

## Regulatory fact rule
Classification, CE/FDA status, intended use, contraindications, warnings and compliance facts cannot be inferred from scientific research.

## AI rule
AI may assist with research organization, drafting, localization, comparison, metadata and QA. AI is not an authoritative source or medical/scientific/legal/regulatory approval.
